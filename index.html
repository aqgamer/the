HydraFacial: The Vortex-Fusion Process

A non-invasive, multi-step skincare treatment for all skin types.

🌀 The Core Technology

The HydraFacial uses a patented Vortex-Fusion delivery system. This unique spiral tip simultaneously allows for non-invasive skin resurfacing while infusing the skin with nutrient-rich serums.

It combines the benefits of microdermabrasion, chemical peels, and deep hydration in one 30-minute session with zero downtime.

The 3 Key Steps of the Treatment

Step 1: Cleanse & Exfoliate

Uncover a new layer of skin.

The HydroPeel tip gently exfoliates the skin, removing dead cells and excess sebum. A blend of cleansing serums is applied to wash away surface buildup.

Step 2: Acid Peel & Extract

Remove debris from pores.

A mild blend of glycolic and salicylic acids loosens dirt and oil without irritation. The vacuum suction then painlessly dislodges and removes blackheads, whiteheads, and impurities from deep within the pores.

Step 3: Hydrate & Fuse

Saturate the skin's surface.

Antioxidants, hyaluronic acid, and peptides are infused into the clean skin to hydrate, nourish, and protect. This seals the beneficial ingredients for a lasting glow.

Benefits & Target Concerns

  • Brighter, more radiant complexion
  • Reduced appearance of fine lines and wrinkles
  • Improved skin texture and tone
  • Minimizing congested/enlarged pores
  • Targeting hyperpigmentation and brown spots
  • Suitable for virtually all skin types (even sensitive)
